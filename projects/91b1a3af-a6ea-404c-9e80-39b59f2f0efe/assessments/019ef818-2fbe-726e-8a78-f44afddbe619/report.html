Actual production costs will vary and are likely to change based on application-specific sizing, performance requirements, real-world usage patterns, selected region, reserved instance or savings plan commitments, and enterprise agreement discounts.

How are these estimates calculated?

Each recommended Azure service is mapped to a specific SKU suitable for a dev/test baseline. Prices are retrieved from the Azure Retail Prices API for the East US region.

Normalization rules:

  • Hourly services (e.g. App Service B1, VMs) — unit price × 730 hours/month
  • Daily services (e.g. SQL Database DTU) — unit price × 30 days/month
  • Consumption services (e.g. Container Apps, Functions) — fixed assumed monthly usage volume
  • Monthly services (e.g. Service Bus Standard) — listed price as-is

No reserved instance, savings plan, or enterprise agreement discounts are applied. The lowest non-zero retail price matching the SKU filter is selected when multiple meter options exist.
